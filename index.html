01 — About
Where supply chain depth
meets AI product thinking

Ten years ago I was reconciling freight manifests and hunting root causes by hand — chasing down why a forecast was wrong before it turned into a stockout. That's where I learned to read a supply chain like a story, not a spreadsheet.

That instinct followed me into AI. I translate between people who rarely agree — planners who trust their gut, engineers who trust their models, executives who just want the answer — and ship products all three can live with.

Right now, that means running two products out of one 12-person pod at UPL: a Multi-Agent Platform giving leadership a real-time view of the business, and a Control Tower that's already freed $22M in working capital. Built on LangChain, LangGraph, n8n, Lovable. Shipped, not slideware.
